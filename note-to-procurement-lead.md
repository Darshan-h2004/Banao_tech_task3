**Re:** Colour Correction System — Proposal Summary

---

### The Problem in One Sentence

Every time your team runs an old design again, the tiles come out the wrong shade — and it currently takes 6 to 7 hours and thousands of wasted square metres to fix it manually. We can solve the calculation part of that problem in under 3 minutes, and eliminate the wasted tiles entirely.

---

### What We Are Proposing

A **lab workstation and software system** — installed in your existing tile lab — that does the following:

1. **Scans a freshly fired sample tile** using a calibrated camera in a standard lighting booth.
2. **Compares it automatically** to your locked original master tile reference.
3. **Tells your QC Technician exactly which ink channels to adjust** (e.g., "increase Brown by 4.2%, reduce Black by 1.8%") — in under 3 minutes, instead of 6–7 hours of Photoshop guesswork.
4. **Requires your QC Technician to review and approve** before any change is sent to the printer. No automatic changes. Your people stay in control.

The system also protects your original master files, which we know have been accumulating small edits over the years, gradually drifting from the original approved shade. We lock the originals and always correct from the true master.

---

### The Workflow Change (Simple)

**Today:** Print sample → fire it → compare visually → guess corrections in Photoshop → make 6–7 variants → fire all of them → compare again → repeat for 6–7 hours while the line produces waste.

**With our system:** Fire one sample tile 90 minutes before the batch starts → scan it in the booth (< 3 minutes) → system gives the exact correction → QC Technician approves → done. Your main production line produces **zero wasted tiles**.

### One Important Honest Note

The system will eliminate the multi-hour Photoshop guessing loop and the wasted tiles at batch changeover. It will **not** eliminate the physical kiln firing time (that is chemistry and physics, not software). Our solution makes the *decision* instant — the kiln still takes 45–90 minutes to fire a sample tile, which is why we recommend sending one test tile through the kiln 90 minutes before your batch starts, so your main production never stops.
